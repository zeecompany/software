📊 THE KEY NUMBERS (as of 4 Sept 2026)
• Budget date: 28 October 2026 (Chancellor John Healey's first)
• Tax rises expected: £10–15bn (i / economists) to £25bn (Capital Economics)
• Fiscal headroom: ~£25bn in Nov 2025 → ~£14bn after this week's gilt sell-off
• 30-year gilt yield: 5.89% – highest since 1998
• Defence to 3% of GDP: ~£28bn a year (Resolution Foundation)
• Already law: income tax thresholds frozen to April 2031 → 4.8m more higher-rate taxpayers (OBR); ≈£39bn/yr (IFS)
• Already law: cash ISA cap £20k → £12k for under-65s from April 2027; pensions inside IHT from April 2027; +2p on dividend, savings and property income
• CGT today: 18% / 24%, £3,000 allowance · Pension tax-free lump sum cap: £268,275

✅ 6 THINGS TO DO BEFORE 28 OCTOBER
1. Use this year's £20,000 ISA allowance
2. Use the £3,000 CGT allowance before Budget day (CGT changes can apply immediately)
3. Bring forward planned lump-sum pension contributions
4. Check where you sit against the £50,270 and £100,000 thresholds
5. Start the 7-year clock on gifts you were already planning
6. Prepare, don't panic – pre-Budget rumours are often wrong
